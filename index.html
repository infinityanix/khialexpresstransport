About us

Founded in Dubai two decades ago, Khial Express Transport LLC has grown into a leading provider of heavy equipment and transport solutions. Under the leadership of our CEO Khial Samand, we have built a reputation for excellence, trust, and professionalism.

We specialize in offering a wide range of transportation and heavy machinery rental services tailored to meet the needs of construction, infrastructure, and industrial projects. Whether it’s moving heavy equipment, providing logistics support, or supplying well-maintained machinery, our goal is to keep your projects running smoothly and on time.
